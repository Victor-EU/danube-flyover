About Danube Flyover

      
A flight along the Budapest riverfront, from the Japanese Garden on Margaret Island to the Central Market Hall.

      
Controls

      
    
        
  • The autopilot flies the tour. Steer to take over: W/S speed, A/D turn, Q/E down and up (as the bird), or drag. After 3 s without input, the autopilot takes back over.
    • 
        
  • Land: hold Q over the water. Take off: hold W at the boat's top speed.
    • 
        
  • Space pauses the tour and leaves you in control. 1–9 and 0 jump to the ten stops. T hides the time slider.
    • 
        
  • Landmark cards: click one to open it, Esc to close it.
    •
