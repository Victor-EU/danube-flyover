About Danube Flyover

      
A flight along the Budapest riverfront, from the Japanese Garden on Margaret Island to the Central Market Hall.

      
Controls

      
    
        
  • The autopilot flies the tour. Steer to take over: W/S speed, A/D turn, Q/E down and up (as the glider), or drag. After 3 s without input, the autopilot takes back over.
    • 
        
  • Land: hold Q over the water. Once you've landed or steered the boat, it stays a boat, the autopilot keeping it to the river, until you take off: hold E (or W at the boat's top speed, or drag up).
    • 
        
  • Boost: hold Shift, or press B or the » button in the bar to keep it on, for three times the speed. It's yours while it's on: the autopilot waits.
    • 
        
  • Space pauses the tour and leaves you in control. 1–9 and 0 jump to the ten stops. T hides the time slider.
    • 
        
  • Landmark cards: click one to open it, Esc to close it.
    • 
        
  • The music is off until you turn it on: the speaker button in the bar, or M.
