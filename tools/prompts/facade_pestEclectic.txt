Facade texture, exactly 4 window bays wide and 4 storeys high, filling the square: the bottom quarter is the ground floor, the three storeys above it must repeat when stacked. One window centred in each bay of each storey. Light, near-neutral plaster and stone (the building's colour is applied later); dark window glass with faint reflections, net curtains behind some panes.
Pest eclectic tenement (1880–1910): rusticated ground floor with arched shop windows and a door, a pedimented first floor, straight hoods above, string courses between storeys.
