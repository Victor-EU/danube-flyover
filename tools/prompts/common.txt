Photographic texture for a realistic 3D flyover of Budapest along the Danube. A real surface photographed straight on in soft, even overcast daylight, as a physically based albedo map: true materials, fine detail, and the wear of a century of city weather (soot in the recesses, rain streaks under sills and cornices, dust, patched repairs, faded paint). Photorealistic: not an illustration, painting or render. No cast shadows, no directional sunlight, no sky, no perspective, no people, no vehicles, no plants, no text, no signs, no logos, no graffiti.
