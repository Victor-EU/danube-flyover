A style sheet for the whole world: a riverside street on the Pest embankment in flat midday light, straight on. A row of five- and six-storey eclectic tenements in cream and ochre plaster, a classicist block with pilasters, a secession facade with a ceramic frieze, terracotta, slate and green copper roofs, a granite quay wall and paving, the dark teal river along the bottom. Along the bottom edge, colour swatches of the palette.
