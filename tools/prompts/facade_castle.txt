Facade texture, exactly 4 window bays wide and 4 storeys high, filling the square: the bottom quarter is the ground floor, the three storeys above it must repeat when stacked. One window centred in each bay of each storey. Near-white neutral plaster (the colour is applied later), dark glass.
Castle District baroque house: segmental-arched window hoods, aprons under the sills, a stone base and an arched gateway.
